APEX EVOLVE Privacy Notice

Last updated: July 25, 2026

APEX EVOLVE does not require an account and does not collect names, chat, payment details, precise location, or keyboard and mouse input.

Optional gameplay analytics

On supported web editions, analytics loads only after you select “Allow Analytics.” It records aggregate gameplay events such as game mode, arena, round reached, score, session number on the same browser, and play duration. Google Analytics processes those events with advertising signals and ad personalization disabled. You can decline analytics and continue playing normally.

Local storage

The game stores your analytics choice and a simple play-session count in your browser. Development builds may also store exported evolution diagnostics. Clearing site data removes these values.

Platform editions and payments

CrazyGames editions use the CrazyGames SDK for platform analytics and advertising; the external funding panel is disabled there. Direct web donations open Stripe or a UPI application, whose own privacy terms apply. Payment credentials are never sent to the game.

Game audio

The web game may retrieve sound files from the Freesound content delivery network. If those files cannot be reached, procedural audio is used instead.

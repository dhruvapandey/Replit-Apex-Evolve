# Combat sound credits

Core weapon and explosion recordings are streamed from Freesound and layered with the game's procedural recoil, debris, pressure, and spatial effects. All four recordings are published under Creative Commons Zero (CC0).

- “Cannon shot” by SamsterBirdies: https://freesound.org/s/467883/
- “Realistic Gun-Fire” by Mr_KeybOred: https://freesound.org/s/414023/
- “Artillery Gunfire” by qubodup: https://freesound.org/s/239137/
- “Explosion.wav” by cejordi84: https://freesound.org/s/232398/
